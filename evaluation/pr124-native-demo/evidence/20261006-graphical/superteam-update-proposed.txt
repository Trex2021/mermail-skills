Freelance Margin Guard compares the owner-selected accepted scope with a later request in Mermail. It preserves exclusions and acceptance criteria, accounts for remaining included revisions, and builds a source-linked change ledger, owner-grounded cost ranges and three client options. Missing rates remain unpriced; email instructions and funding receipts cannot authorize work, sends or payment.

Primary demo: https://x.com/Ehsan_Benvari/status/2107520796780630361
Continuous English recording of a graphical evaluation workspace driven by the actual GitHub Copilot client and hosted Mermail tool events. The agent reads only the two selected synthetic sources, invokes the shipped packet builder, freezes the complete exact draft, stops before any write, saves one internal self-addressed draft under owner-delegated operator permission, and reads the complete stored body back. No external send, payment, wallet or accepted agreement. This is an evaluation UI, not the official Mermail console.

The live hypothetical scenario produces 15–22 added hours, $352.50–$517 ordinary fees and $387.75–$568.70 including a 10% owner-supplied rush rule. One remaining included revision is not charged twice. These are test estimates, not customer revenue or guaranteed dates. The old offline benchmark (26–33 hours, $390–$495 base) has distinct inputs.
